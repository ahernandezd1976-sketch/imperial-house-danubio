### Idea 1: Imperial Parchment & Gilt — "Habsburg Neo-Classicism"
**Design Movement:** 19th-century European royal court aesthetics fused with editorial luxury print design.
**Core Principles:**
- Aged parchment textures as primary surface, with gold leaf accents as structural dividers
- Asymmetric editorial layouts: large typographic headers offset against portrait imagery
- Strict typographic hierarchy using a serif display face for titles and a humanist serif for body
- Ornamental borders and filigree as section dividers, not just decoration

**Color Philosophy:** Ivory/parchment (#F5EDD8) backgrounds, deep imperial navy (#1A2744), burnished gold (#C9A84C), and crimson (#8B1A1A) as accent. Evokes candlelight and velvet.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial columns — wide left column for imagery, narrow right column for text. Sections alternate orientation. No uniform grid.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin gold horizontal rules with central heraldic ornament
- Drop caps in gold for section openers
- Wax seal motifs as section markers

**Interaction Philosophy:** Slow, deliberate hover transitions — as if turning pages of an illuminated manuscript.
**Animation:** Fade-in with 600ms ease-out for sections; gold underlines grow from left on hover; no bouncy motion.
**Typography System:** Playfair Display (headings) + EB Garamond (body) — classical, authoritative, literary.

### Idea 2: Imperial Modernism — "Vienna Secession Reborn"
**Design Movement:** Vienna Secession (1897) meets contemporary Swiss editorial design.
**Core Principles:**
- Geometric ornament derived from Klimt-era motifs (squares, spirals, interlocking borders)
- High contrast: jet black sections alternating with cream panels
- Typography as architecture — oversized display text used structurally
- Gold as the only accent color, used sparingly and precisely

**Color Philosophy:** Near-black (#0D0D0D), warm cream (#F7F2E8), and 24-karat gold (#D4AF37). Stark, luxurious, timeless.

**Layout Paradigm:** Horizontal banding — full-width sections stacked vertically, each with a distinct visual weight. Odd sections are dark, even sections are light.

**Signature Elements:**
- Klimt-inspired geometric border patterns as section dividers (SVG)
- Large Roman numerals as section counters
- Monogram "AD" (Austria-Danubio) as a recurring brand mark

**Interaction Philosophy:** Precise, architectural — hover states reveal gold outlines, no softness.
**Animation:** Sections slide up 20px on scroll-enter with 400ms ease-out; gold borders draw in on hover.
**Typography System:** Cormorant Garamond (display, very large, thin weight) + Source Serif 4 (body) — elegant tension between fragility and authority.

### Idea 3: Official Imperial Portal — "Regal Gravitas"
**Design Movement:** Contemporary official state/monarchy website (inspired by British Royal Family, Monaco Royal House) with deep historical roots.
**Core Principles:**
- Dark imperial navy as the dominant background — authoritative, nocturnal, serious
- Gold and ivory as the only accent tones — restraint communicates power
- Structured but not rigid: a clear top navigation, hero section, and modular content cards
- Photography and portraiture as primary visual anchors

**Color Philosophy:** Deep navy (#0F1B35), imperial gold (#B8960C), ivory (#F0EAD6), and slate white (#E8E8E8). Conveys legitimacy and permanence.

**Layout Paradigm:** Top navigation with coat of arms logo; full-bleed hero with palace image; alternating full-width and two-column content sections; footer with dynasty motto.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin gold horizontal dividers with centered ornamental diamond
- Subtle damask/brocade texture overlay on dark sections
- Portrait cards with gold-bordered frames

**Interaction Philosophy:** Dignified — hover states use gold color shifts, no playful effects.
**Animation:** Fade-up entrance for content sections; gold underline grows on nav hover.
**Typography System:** Cinzel (headings — Roman inscriptional) + Crimson Text (body — warm, readable serif).
